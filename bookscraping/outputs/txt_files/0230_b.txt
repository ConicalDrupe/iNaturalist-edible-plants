QUICK CHECK: Vetch with very
widely spaced leaflets and long Memos
of small pala flowers.
DESCRIPTION: Perennial, the stems very
thin and up to 3' (1 m) high, hairless or
nearly so. Leaves with 8-16 leaflets and a
tendril that is usually unbranched. Leaflets narrowly elliptic,
sometimes almost linear, 0.4-1.1" (10-28 mm) ong, often
deeply folded along the midvein, usually bristle tipped, the
surface with very fine hair. Secondary veins are visible or the
bottom but obscure on the top; often at very low angles. Stip-
ules 3-4 mm long, lanceolate, entire or with one lobe at the base.
Inflorescence a one-sided raceme of 8-20 flowers on a long,
faintly hairy peduncle. Flowers 8-12 mm long, very narrow.
the corolla white with purple veins in the center. Pods 0 6-1.2"
(15-30 mm), somewhat flattened, containing 4-9 seeds.
HABITAT: Dry forests or savannahs of oak and pine. soad-
sides, forest margins, especially where sandy and mildly acidic.
Native. Conservation 2/1.
COMMENTS: A tasty and attractive wildflower. this species
is very easy to recognize. I have not eaten the seeds. AKA pale
wood vetch.
